# Mega Stone and nature mint locations

Added 40 visible, one-time Mega Stone pickups: **28 official (70%) and 12 custom (30%)**.
The ratio applies to the new world pickups; shop inventories remain complete.
Added **42 nature mints**, with two copies of every one of the 21 mint varieties.
These additions span **51 maps**, from Route 102 and Petalburg Woods to Victory Road and the Orange Islands.

Existing items and NPCs retain their original local IDs. New pickups use ordinary item-ball scripts and independent save flags, so collected items stay collected and a full bag does not consume the pickup. No save expansion is required.

## Shops

- Lilycove Department Store 3F: all nature mints remain available.
- Lilycove Department Store 4F Mega Stone clerk: choose **YES** for custom stones or **NO** for the existing official selection. All 67 custom stones are available.
- Orange City Mart: all existing custom stones remain available.
