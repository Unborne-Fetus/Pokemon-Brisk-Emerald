# Curated Elite Four and Wallace teams

Every member is **level 100 on Normal, Hard and Extreme**, or **70 on Easy**.
Sidney, Phoebe and Glacia use native doubles; Drake and Wallace use singles.
The battle-format option can still override the normal format.

All 30 members have held items, explicit abilities, matching natures and deliberate
EV spreads. These five teams retain their authored sets across modes, including
Protect, screens, recovery and speed control. Easy still uses reduced IV/EV training
and simpler AI. Hard/Extreme retain the authored role spreads and their stronger AI.

Moves are checked against the repository’s full cross-generation learnability
catalog, including legitimate TM, tutor and transfer moves; they are not restricted
to the subset of TMs currently sold in Hoenn. Native tests validate actual abilities,
held-item restrictions, safe attacks in native doubles and generated set preservation.

## Sidney

Grimmsnarl and Tyranitar lead the native double battle: Prankster screens buy time for Mega Tyranitar, while Taunt disrupts setup and Trick Room. Water coverage pressures Ground/Rock/Fire, Spirit Break and Steel/Poison coverage threaten Fighting/Fairy attackers, and Guts Obstagoon turns burns into offense. Kingambit is explicitly reserved as the last ace for Supreme Overlord and Sucker Punch. Tyranitar uses Rock Slide rather than ally-hitting Earthquake.

## Phoebe

Flutter Mane supplies fast Icy Wind and spread Fairy damage against Dark teams. Annihilape punishes Intimidate, ignores powder with Safety Goggles, and threatens Normal/Dark/Steel targets with Drain Punch. Gholdengo has status immunity and an Air Balloon for Ground attacks. Unaware Skeledirge checks physical setup, burns attackers and can Tera Fairy against Dark pressure. Gengar has coherent special coverage; Infiltrator Dragapult supplies a physical finisher against screens and Substitute.

## Glacia

Alolan Ninetales replaces Walrein and leads beside Baxcalibur. Snow and Aurora Veil provide protection while Encore disrupts passive/setup turns. Baxcalibur cannot be burned; it and Mamoswine have single-target Ground coverage against Steel/Fire/Rock. Bundle provides Icy Wind, and Bundle/Lapras use Freeze-Dry against Water teams. Lapras is specially trained with Water Absorb and Weakness Policy, with G-Max Resonance as another route to Veil. Scarf Dracovish pressures Fire/Rock, removes screens with Psychic Fangs and can Tera Water; Ice Shard supplies priority.

## Wallace

Garchomp supplies Stealth Rock, Greninja supplies Scarf revenge killing and Grass Knot against bulky Water/Ground types, and Corviknight supplies recovery, hazard removal and a physical pivot. Unaware Skeledirge stops stat-boost sweepers; its burn/Hex combination and defensive Tera Fairy punish Dark pressure. Sylveon supplies Pixilate Hyper Voice, Throat Spray, Wish/Protect and Mystical Fire for Steel coverage. Mega Metagross closes with priority Bullet Punch and Ice Punch against Dragons/Ground types.
