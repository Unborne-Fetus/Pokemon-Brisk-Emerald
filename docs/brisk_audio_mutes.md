# Independent audio mutes

Options page 7 has three independent ON/OFF controls:

- **MUTE SOUND:** menu clicks, movement, battle and environmental sound effects.
- **MUTE MUSIC:** background music, fanfares and the Bard's musical voice samples.
- **MUTE CRIES:** Pokémon cries, including both simultaneous cry players.

All three default to OFF (audio enabled) and persist with the game save. Stereo/
mono remains a separate choice on Options page 1. Mutes take effect on current
audio and newly started sounds; music resumes at its current position on unmute.

The mixer silences output rather than stopping players. Song progress, fades,
cry playback and fanfare/script waits continue normally. Fanfares are classified
as music even though the engine plays them through a sound-effect player.
