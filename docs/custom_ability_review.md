# Custom ability review

Abilities were reviewed against the supplied artwork, existing lore and battle role.
Existing engine abilities are used. Fitting traits such as Strong Jaw on fanged
predators, Tough Claws on the clawed Duneshred, and Iron Fist on visible fists
are retained. Stats, types, sprites, evolution methods and moves are unchanged.

This pass changes **42 forms**. All three Mega ability slots agree;
ordinary Fakémon retain three selectable ability slots. Mega choices also account
for their required held stone. Existing Pokémon adopt
the revised ability for their saved slot without a save migration.
